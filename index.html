RISK SCENARIO 1 Scenario 1: A 7-Trade Losing Streak Probability: Normal across 300+ trades

With a 50% win rate, it is completely normal to see 6 to 8 losses in a row at some point during a full year. During this streak, account balance dropped by about -7.8% (from ₹1,00,000 down to ₹92,200).

⚠️ Common Client Mistake: Turning off the bot out of worry after 5 or 6 losses — right before the strategy enters a series of winning trades that recovers the loss.
🛡️ How the Strategy Recovers: Because our average winning trade is about twice the size of a loss (1.96:1 reward-to-risk), the bot needs only 4 winning trades to fully recover from 7 losses and reach a new high.
RISK SCENARIO 2 Scenario 2: Sideways & Choppy Markets Market Condition: Sideways / Range-Bound

Trend-following systems perform best when the market is clearly moving up or down. When Bitcoin stays flat in a tight range for weeks, prices can bounce back and forth without momentum, causing small stop-outs.

⚠️ Real Example (May 2026): During flat market conditions in May 2026, the bot took 14 trades (5 wins, 9 losses) and finished the month down -3.2% — the worst month of the year.
🛡️ Built-in Protection: The bot checks higher timeframes and volatility before entering to filter out sideways chop. If a trade stays flat for 18 hours, it exits automatically to keep capital safe.
RISK SCENARIO 3 Scenario 3: Waiting for New Highs (Recovery Periods) Longest Period: March 12 – April 10

In trend trading, profits often come in strong multi-week moves, followed by quiet periods where the account stays slightly below its peak while waiting for fresh setups.

⚠️ What to Expect: Returns do not come in a straight line every week. The account may stay flat for 2 to 3 weeks, and then make a strong move over the next few days.
🛡️ Recommended Approach: Review performance over 3-month (quarterly) periods rather than day-to-day. Over any 3-month window in testing, the strategy was profitable 98.7% of the time.
RISK SCENARIO 4 Scenario 4: Fast Market Moves & Slippage Fast Market Moves

During sudden market drops (like Bitcoin dropping sharply in a few minutes on breaking news), prices can move very fast. A stop loss set at -1.5% may occasionally fill at -2.0% due to fast-moving prices.

⚠️ Key Risk: Smaller altcoins can see wider price jumps than Bitcoin or Ethereum during sudden market dips.
🛡️ Built-in Protection: If Bitcoin moves more than 1.5% in 15 minutes, the bot pauses all new trade entries for 30 minutes until the market calms down.
